   Nino Gugushvili-Mikaberidze" - http://bpg.sytes.net

It is a LEGACY Georgian font and that is the whole of why site.css
declares it the way it does: it draws MTAVRULI CAPITALS at the MKHEDRULI
code points (U+10D0-10FF) and has nothing at all at the Mtavruli block
Unicode 11 added (U+1C90-1CBF). So the page keeps its Georgian in
ordinary lower-case Mkhedruli - which is what copy, search and a screen
reader want - and the capitals on titles are the font drawing them, not
the text being changed. Converting the text to real Mtavruli would push
every title straight off this font and back onto Noto.

Covers all 33 modern letters and the punctuation this page sets in
titles, except U+00B2 (²) and U+2116 (№), which fall back to Jost.
